How it is made

Four steps.
All by hand.

Plastic is collected from landfills, rivers and tuckshops, then worked into every piece by hand.

01

Collect

Discarded plastic gathered from landfills, rivers and tuckshops.

02

Clean

Every packet is cleaned before it is used.

03

Cut

Packets are cut ready for weaving.

04

Weave

Woven by hand into mats, fashion and decor.

One mat takes between 500 and 800 discarded chip packets.

Our story

It started with a grandmother’s craft.

Umlazi Art and Recycling is a registered business founded by Minenhle Simelane, 25, from Umlazi, south of Durban.

Roots

Learnt from his grandmother

Minenhle learnt traditional mat sewing from his grandmother. These mats are used for cultural ceremonies and by members of the Shembe church.

First mats

Chip packets, shared 2,000+ times

His first creations were colourful woven mats made from discarded chip packets. A Facebook post of them was shared more than 2,000 times within hours.

2015

Plastic meets fashion

From 2015 he began mixing plastic into fashion.

“I asked myself: how can I turn waste into something valuable?”
Minenhle Simelane, founder News24, 20 July 2026.
